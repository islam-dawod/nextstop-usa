Understanding the main routes

Work routes

Categories tied to employment, skills or talent. Conditions and employer involvement vary by route.

Study routes

For accepted students and exchange visitors, usually with proof of admission and funding.

Visitor routes

Short stays for tourism or business, where purpose, ties and trip length matter.

Other routes

Family, humanitarian and special categories, each with its own criteria and process.
